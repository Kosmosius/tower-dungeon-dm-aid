Credits and source notice

The Crown adventure and GEWINN game were created by Marshall Alexander. This page identifies the sources and changes used by the current app. It remains available without a network connection after the shell is installed.

The Crown

The adventure source is the locked The Crown v1.1.1 Razor Edition: Book I, World and Campaign; Book II, Areas; Book III, Ways; and Book IV, People, Monsters and Treasure. The four masters and their print PDFs remain unchanged. This DM Aid includes a line-preserving GEWINN-only reading projection, locality and Way records, and the author's maps. The alternate B/X–OSE rules interface is omitted.

Marshall Alexander authorized publication of Crown and GEWINN material they own. The Crown narrative and author-created maps have their own rights status. The GEWINN license below is not a license for Crown prose, maps or application code.

Changes and Crown-specific rulings

The app turns source procedures into choices, calculated outcomes and persistent local campaign state. It does not amend the locked Crown books or the general GEWINN migration bundle. The author set these Crown-app rules explicitly:

  • D15: all eight GEWINN wound tables in the 10 October 2026 text are the Crown app's table authority. Wound Value bands are 1–3 Minor, 4–6 Serious, 7–9 Grave, 10–15 Mortal and 16+ Catastrophic. A later same-type result adds to established lasting injury.
  • Grave Ability Loss is 1d2; Mortal Ability Loss is 1d4. Sacrifice's maximum Wound Value by levels 1–5 is 3 / 6 / 9 / 15 / 16.
  • Crown clinical time uses the 30-minute Turn.

This bundle supports the DM reading and campaign tools. Solo automation remains incomplete; some encounters and keyed actions still require a referee ruling.
